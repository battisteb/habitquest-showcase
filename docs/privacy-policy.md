# HabitQuest — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 15, 2026*

HabitQuest ("the app") is published by **Battiste Boungo** ("we"). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: **batto060504@gmail.com**.

## 1. Data we collect

| Data | Why | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Email address and password (hashed) | Create and secure your account | Supabase (authentication) |
| If you choose "Continue with Google": the email address, name and profile picture link Google shares | Create and sign in to your account (we keep the email; the app does not use your Google name or picture) | Supabase (authentication) |
| Username, avatar colors, level, XP, gold, equipped items | Run the game and show your hero to your friends | Supabase |
| Habits, completions, streaks, optional completion notes | Core habit-tracking features | Supabase, and cached on your device |
| Your mood of the day (1 to 5), only if you log it | Show you which habits go with a better mood, in your own stats; never shared or shown to anyone else | Supabase |
| Only if you use Pip, the AI coach (Premium): your quest names, schedules, reasons, anchors and mini versions, the days you did them over the last 4 weeks, your streaks, your moods and completion notes of the last 14 days, and what you write to Pip | Generate Pip's advice, when you open one of his cards or write to him | Sent to Anthropic to generate the answer; the answers and your conversation stay on your device; the number of requests per day is counted on Supabase |
| Friends, duels, challenges, in-app notifications | Social features | Supabase |
| Time zone (e.g. Europe/Paris) and app language | Count streaks and daily limits on your local day; send notifications and e-mails in your language | Supabase |
| Platform you last used (web, iPhone or Android) and when you last opened the app | Aggregate usage statistics (e.g. how many players use Android); never shown to other players | Supabase |
| In-app activity events, only once we turn this feature on: for example that you opened the Premium screen and from where, started a trial or a purchase, or viewed a recap; and which version of a screen you saw when we compare two versions (A/B test) | Understand what helps players and improve the app; never shown to other players, never sold or shared | Supabase |
| Push notification token | Send reminders and game notifications | Supabase |
| Notification settings: reminder times, tone, quiet hours, friends' notifications on or off | Send reminders at the right time, never during your quiet hours | Supabase, and on your device |
| Messages you send to Support, with the app version, platform and language | Answer you and fix problems | Supabase |
| On gethabitquest.com, only if you join the waitlist: your email address, the site language and your consent | Send you one e-mail when the apps are available | Supabase |
| Purchase history and subscription status | Unlock Premium | RevenueCat, Apple / Google |
| Advertising identifier (IDFA / Android advertising ID) — **only if you allow tracking** | Show ads to free users | Google AdMob |

When the app or the website hits an error, a technical report is sent to Sentry so we can fix it: the error and where it happened in the code, the app version, the device or browser model and the operating system. These reports contain no name, e-mail, habits or messages (personal data collection is turned off). We do not use third-party analytics SDKs (the activity events above stay on our own servers), and we do not sell your data.

### Pip, the AI coach (Premium)

Pip is an artificial intelligence. When you open one of his cards or write to him, and only after you have agreed in the app, the data listed above is sent to Anthropic, which writes his answer with its Claude model. Your e-mail address and username are not sent. Anthropic acts as our processor: it uses this data only to answer, and under its commercial terms it does not use it to train its models ([privacy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)). Anthropic is based in the United States; the transfer is covered by its data processing terms, which include the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Pip only suggests: a change to your quests is made only when you tap it. His answers can be wrong and are not medical advice. You can withdraw your agreement at any time from Pip's page; nothing is sent to Anthropic after that.

## 2. Advertising and tracking

The app shows no banners and no imposed full-screen ads. Free users can choose to watch a rewarded ad served by Google AdMob, for example to earn a streak freeze: no ad plays unless you ask for one. Rewarded ads are currently available on Android only. If they come to iOS, the app will ask your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before any personalized ad; if you decline, only non-personalized ads are requested. Premium users see no ads. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. Third-party processors

- **Supabase** — database, authentication and server functions ([privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy))
- **Anthropic** — Pip's answers (AI coach, Premium), only if you use it ([privacy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy))
- **RevenueCat** — subscription management ([privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy))
- **Google AdMob** — advertising ([privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))
- **Sentry** — error and crash reports, stored in the European Union ([privacy](https://sentry.io/privacy/))
- **Expo** — push notification delivery and website hosting ([privacy](https://expo.dev/privacy))
- **Resend** — account e-mails such as password reset ([privacy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy))
- **Apple / Google** — payments through the App Store and Google Play
- **Google** — sign-in, only if you choose "Continue with Google" ([privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))

## 4. Who can see your data

Other players can find you by username and see your public profile: username, avatar and equipped items, level, XP, rank, gold, best streak and sign-up date (global leaderboard, friend search). Your friends also see your duel and challenge results. Your habits, notes, time zone, subscription and Support messages are private. Your conversation with Pip is private too.

## 5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. Pip's answers and your conversation with him are kept on your device and erased when you sign out; Anthropic keeps what it receives only for the limited time set by its terms. Waitlist addresses are used once, for the launch e-mail, then deleted; you can ask us to remove yours earlier at any time. You can delete your account at any time from **Settings → Delete my account**: your account and all associated data are permanently erased from our servers. An active subscription must be cancelled separately in your App Store or Google Play settings.

## 6. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to its processing. Contact us at **batto060504@gmail.com**. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France: the CNIL).

## 7. Children

HabitQuest is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in South Korea, where the Personal Information Protection Act sets that age) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## 8. Changes

We will update this page if the policy changes and change the "Last updated" date above.
